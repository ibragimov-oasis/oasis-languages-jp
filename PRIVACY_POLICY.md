## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how oasis-languages-jp (the **"Service"**) collects, uses, and safeguards information, and sets forth strict protections against unauthorized third-party harvesting.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal user data.
- We do not share telemetry, user interactions, or database records with third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or AI model training consortiums.

## 3. Strict Prohibition on Third-Party Data Scraping & Harvesting
Any automated harvesting of user profiles, email addresses, public posts, transaction data, or system metadata by third parties, crawlers, or AI training bots is strictly prohibited under our [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md).

## 4. Local Storage & Client-Side Processing
Where applicable, client-side preferences (such as UI themes, localized settings, or temporary workspace states) are stored exclusively in your local browser storage (`localStorage` / `sessionStorage`) and are not transmitted to foreign external servers.

## 5. Security Measures
We implement modern technical and organizational safeguards to protect our codebase, backend APIs, and database assets against unauthorized access, alteration, or interception.
