## 1. Introduction & Scope

## 3. Categories of Data Processed
We strictly adhere to the principle of **Data Minimization**:
1. **Technical Telemetry (Essential):** IP address (anonymized where feasible), browser type, operating system, and timestamp required for server security, load balancing, and DDoS mitigation.
2. **Client-Side Preferences:** UI theme (dark/light), locale, and workspace state stored locally in your browser (`localStorage`).
3. **User-Provided Information (if applicable):** Direct communications, feedback, or credentials explicitly submitted by the user.
4. **Data We DO NOT Collect:** We do NOT collect sensitive biometric data, racial/ethnic data, political opinions, or financial credentials unless explicitly required by an enterprise contract.

## 4. Lawful Bases for Processing (EU / UK GDPR Art. 6)
We process personal data only when a recognized lawful basis applies:
- **Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)):** To deliver core platform functionality and fulfill user requests.
- **Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)):** To protect system security, prevent fraud, mitigate abuse, and optimize platform reliability.
- **Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)):** For optional features, cookies, or direct communications, where consent has been freely given and may be revoked at any time.

## 5. Rights of European & UK Data Subjects (GDPR Arts. 15-22)
Users in the European Union and United Kingdom enjoy comprehensive rights:
1. **Right of Access (Art. 15):** Request confirmation of processing and obtain a copy of your personal data.
2. **Right to Rectification (Art. 16):** Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
3. **Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Art. 17):** Request permanent deletion of your data when no longer necessary.
4. **Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18):** Restrict how your data is processed under specific legal grounds.
5. **Right to Data Portability (Art. 20):** Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON/CSV).
6. **Right to Object (Art. 21):** Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
7. **Right to Lodge a Complaint (Art. 77):** Lodge a complaint with a competent EU Data Protection Supervisory Authority (e.g., CNIL in France, BfDI in Germany, DPC in Ireland).

## 6. Notice for California & US Residents (CCPA / CPRA)
- **Zero Sale of Personal Data:** We do **NOT** sell, rent, monetize, or share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.140).
- **Your California Rights:** California residents have the right to request disclosure of categories of personal information collected, request deletion, correct inaccurate data, and exercise these rights free from discrimination.
