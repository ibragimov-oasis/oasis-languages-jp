## 1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?
Cookies and browser Local Storage (`localStorage` / `sessionStorage`) are small text files or key-value entries stored directly on your computer or mobile device by your web browser when you visit a website.

## 2. Categories of Storage We Use
1. **Strictly Necessary (Essential):**
   - **Purpose:** Required for site navigation, security verification, state persistence, and authentication.
   - **Legal Basis:** Exempt from prior consent under EU ePrivacy Directive Art. 5(3) as strictly necessary for delivering the requested service.
2. **Functional & User Preferences:**
   - **Purpose:** Storing your UI appearance preferences (Dark/Light mode), localized language preferences, or active workspace settings.
   - **Storage:** Client-side only in your local browser; never transmitted to advertising networks.
3. **Third-Party Trackers:**
   - **We DO NOT use:** Third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, data broker beacons, or behavioral profiling cookies.

## 3. How You Can Control Cookies
You have full control over cookies in your browser:
- You can block, disable, or delete cookies via your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, Mobile Safari).
- Clearing your browser cache and local storage immediately removes all stored client preferences.
